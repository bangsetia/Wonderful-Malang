Experience the beauty and the beautiful chaos of East Java's streets in the most immersive yet hilarious driving simulator ever created!
Wonderful Malang is a massive-scale bus and public transport simulator built entirely from scratch on the Semarbroka Engine, a custom C++ game engine utilizing DirectX 11 PBR (Physically Based Rendering). Explore a precise 1:1 scale digital replica of Greater Malang to Surabaya, constructed using real-world spatial GIS data (SHP).
Behind its stunning, high-fidelity visuals lies a hardcore driver survival simulation packed with unforgiving, laugh-out-loud local street behaviors!

KEY FEATURES:

• 1:1 Real-World Scale & 24/7 Time Cycle: Drive through authentic routes connecting Malang and Surabaya. Experience a true-to-life 24/7 cycle with dynamic weather. Navigate dense night fog on the extreme mountain roads of Poncokusumo towards Mount Bromo, or endure the peak-hour gridlock of local markets.
• Driver Survival Mechanics: You are not a machine! Step out of your vehicle to refuel, visit local roadside shops to eat and drink, use public restrooms, or rent a cabin on the ferry to sleep and manage your stamina.
• Tourism & Intercity Bus Modes: Take the wheel of legendary Indonesian buses, featured under witty parody names inspired by famous East Java transport companies. Choose the high-speed toll road via Karanglo or the dramatic, traffic-heavy lower route via Lawang.
• Angkot (Public Minivan) Simulator: Experience the true hustle of driving a traditional blue Mikrolet on real routes (AL, GL, ADL, MK). Deal with unpredictable passenger spawning—they will hail you by pointing their finger from the tightest blind spots or steepest hills!
• Highly "Emotional" AI Traffic: Braking instantly for a passenger without using turn signals? Prepare to be aggressively overtaken by angry AI drivers who will yell authentic, localized East Javanese profanities via spatial 3D audio!
• Built-in Map & Mod Editor: Access the comprehensive Level Editor right from the main menu. Effortlessly draw PBR spline roads with your mouse, import your own regional SHP files, place assets, and build your dream custom simulation routes like a sandbox game.
• Real-Time Sea Crossings: Embark on true-to-life maritime journeys across the Bali Strait (2-3 hours) or the Java Sea (up to 24 hours). Choose between an instant Skip Mode or fully explore the ferry's decks on foot while watching the dynamic PBR sunset over the open ocean.
Discover the ultimate crossover between premium custom engine technology and unfiltered local street humor found nowhere else in the world!
